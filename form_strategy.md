# Business Data Collection Strategy

## Form Strategy Based on Business Stages and Data Sensitivity

### 1. Business Stages Overview
- **Stage 1: Early Stage Startups** (0-2 years, <$500K revenue)
- **Stage 2: Growing Businesses** (2-5 years, $500K-$2M revenue)
- **Stage 3: Established Businesses** (5+ years, $2M+ revenue)
- **Stage 4: Mature Enterprises** (10+ years, $10M+ revenue)

### 2. Tiered Form Strategy

#### Tier 1: Initial Contact Form (All Businesses)
This form is sent to any business that contacts your consulting service. It focuses on basic information and general business health:
- Business name, contact details
- Industry and business model
- Years in operation
- Current business challenges
- Goals for next 12 months
- Preferred communication method

#### Tier 2: Business Overview Form (Follow-up)
Sent after initial contact, customized based on business stage:
- Core operations questions
- Size and structure information
- Revenue range (general)
- Primary technology used
- Growth aspirations

#### Tier 3: Detailed Diagnostic Form (Qualified Leads)
Sent to businesses that have shown serious interest and engagement, with sensitivity-based customization:
- Financial metrics (appropriate to business stage)
- Technical infrastructure details
- Customer and market data
- Specific pain points
- Competitive landscape

### 3. Data Sensitivity Classification

#### Low Sensitivity (Always Requestable)
- Business name and contact information
- General industry
- Number of employees
- Years in operation
- General business goals

#### Medium Sensitivity (Request Based on Relationship)
- Revenue ranges (not exact figures)
- Marketing spend
- Customer acquisition metrics
- Technology stack
- Major challenges

#### High Sensitivity (Request After Trust Building)
- Specific revenue figures
- Detailed financial data
- Exact customer numbers
- Profit margins
- Legal or compliance details

### 4. Industry-Specific Form Customization

#### For E-commerce/B2C:
- Customer demographics
- Average order value
- Shopping cart abandonment rates
- Customer lifetime value
- Seasonal trends

#### For SaaS/Software:
- Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue (MRR/ARR)
- Customer churn rate
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- User engagement metrics
- Integration requirements

#### For Professional Services:
- Service offerings
- Client retention rate
- Billable hours
- Team structure
- Project management tools

#### For Manufacturing/Retail:
- Supply chain details
- Inventory turnover
- Cost of goods sold
- Distribution channels
- Quality control processes

### 5. Progressive Profiling Strategy

#### Phase 1: Warm-up (Week 1-2)
- Basic company information
- General business goals
- Current tools and software
- Top 3 business challenges

#### Phase 2: Engagement (Week 3-4)
- Detailed operations questions
- Market positioning
- Customer-related questions
- Growth plans

#### Phase 3: Deep Dive (Week 5-6)
- Financial metrics (appropriate level)
- Competitive analysis
- Strategic initiatives
- Technology needs

### 6. Form Delivery Strategy

#### Channel 1: Email
- Best for initial and secondary forms
- Allows for detailed explanations
- Can be scheduled based on engagement

#### Channel 2: Website Portal
- For businesses that prefer direct interaction
- Allows for progress saving
- Enables real-time validation

#### Channel 3: Integration (Advanced)
- For mature businesses
- Direct API connections
- Automated data collection
- Real-time data updates

### 7. Incentive Structure for Completion

#### Small Incentives
- Free market analysis report
- Industry benchmarking data
- Resource download access
- Exclusive content access

#### Medium Incentives
- Free consultation session
- Custom business health check
- Competitive analysis
- Technology recommendation

#### Large Incentives
- Free initial audit (first month)
- Strategic planning session
- Custom reporting dashboard
- Priority consultation access
