# ABetWorks.in — Country-Wise Industry Issues & Consulting Approach
## Global Consulting Playbook: Region-Specific Challenges & Solutions

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

> "Country to country, issues are different — so our reach and consulting should be according to that."

Every market has unique:
- **Regulatory frameworks** (compliance, licensing, taxation)
- **Cultural business norms** (decision-making, hierarchy, relationships)
- **Infrastructure realities** (digital, physical, financial)
- **Economic pressures** (inflation, currency, credit access)
- **Technology maturity** (adoption rates, digital readiness)

ABetWorks must adapt its consulting methodology, pricing, and deliverables per region.

### India-Specific Industry Pain Points

#### Manufacturing
- **Power costs**: Industrial electricity ₹8-12/unit (vs. ₹4-6 in competing nations)
- **Land acquisition**: Average 18-24 months for manufacturing setup
- **Skill gap**: 70%+ workforce lacks formal vocational training
- **Cluster isolation**: Most MSMEs operate outside organized industrial clusters
- **Family business transition**: 80%+ are founder/family-run with no succession plan

#### Healthcare
- **Doctor shortage**: 1 doctor per 1,500 people (WHO recommends 1:1,000)
- **Tier 2-3 gap**: 80% of specialists concentrated in top 8 cities
- **Insurance penetration**: Only 37% of population covered by any health insurance
- **Medical device regulation**: CDSCO compliance now mandatory for all devices
- **Biomedical waste**: New rules impose heavy penalties for non-compliance

#### Retail/D2C
- **Quick commerce disruption**: 10-minute delivery reshaping customer expectations
- **Open Network (ONDC)**: Government push for decentralized e-commerce
- **FDI restrictions**: Complex multi-brand retail rules limiting foreign investment
- **Consumer Protection Act 2019**: Stricter product liability, e-commerce specific rules
- **Counterfeit/returns fraud**: 5-8% of returns are fraudulent

#### Fintech/BFSI
- **Delinquency spike**: Personal loan 90-day DPD at 3.6% (highest in 6 quarters)
- **NBFC-MFI stress**: RBI reduced qualifying asset requirement from 75% to 60%
- **Video KYC drop-off**: 50-60% abandonment rate
- **UPI dominance**: 14B+ monthly transactions creating new infrastructure needs
- **Account Aggregator**: New consent-based data sharing ecosystem

### India Consulting Approach
- **Language**: Hindi/regional language support essential for Tier 2-3 clients
- **Pricing**: Value-based but MSME-affordable (₹75K-8L range)
- **Delivery**: Hybrid (in-person for discovery + remote for analysis/delivery)
- **Relationships**: Trust-first culture; personal connections matter more than credentials
- **Payment**: 50% advance + milestone-based; delayed payments common (30-60 days)
- **Decision-making**: Often founder-driven; need to convince 1 person, not a committee

| **Africa** | Political instability + currency devaluation | Short engagement cycles; payment in USD; upfront billing |
