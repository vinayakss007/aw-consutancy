# Comprehensive Business Data Collection Questionnaire

## 1. Basic Information
1. What is your business name?
2. What is your business email address?
3. What is your business phone number?
4. Where is your business located (city, state)?
5. What is your business website URL?
6. How long has your business been operating?
7. What is your job title/role in the company?
8. What is your direct contact information?

## 2. Business Overview & Operations
9. What industry does your business operate in?
10. What products or services does your business offer?
11. Who is your target customer demographic?
12. How many employees do you currently have?
13. What is your organizational structure like?
14. What is your average monthly customer acquisition rate?
15. What are your main business goals for the next 12 months?
16. What are your biggest business challenges right now?
17. What differentiates your business from competitors?
18. Where do you see your business in 5 years?

## 3. Financial Data
19. What is your estimated annual revenue range? (Choose: <$100K, $100K-$500K, $500K-$1M, $1M-$5M, $5M+)
20. What is your monthly recurring revenue (MRR)?
21. What are your primary sources of revenue?
22. What is your typical customer acquisition cost (CAC)?
23. What is your customer lifetime value (CLV)?
24. What are your biggest business expenses?
25. What is your current burn rate (if applicable)?
26. Have you raised any funding or investment? (Y/N)
27. What is your current profit margin?
28. Do you have any financial projections for the next 2 years?

## 4. Technical Infrastructure
29. What technology stack does your business currently use?
30. What software tools do you use for daily operations?
31. Do you have a CRM system? (If yes, which one?)
32. How do you store and manage your customer data?
33. What is your current approach to cybersecurity?
34. Do you use cloud-based solutions? (If yes, which provider?)
35. How do you handle data backup and recovery?
36. What systems do you use for accounting/finance?
37. How do you track and measure business performance?
38. Do you have an e-commerce platform? (If yes, which one?)

## 5. Marketing & Sales Data
39. What marketing channels do you currently use?
40. What is your monthly marketing budget?
41. What is your most effective marketing channel?
42. What is your customer conversion rate?
43. How do you track and measure marketing ROI?
44. What is your sales cycle length?
45. What sales tools or platforms do you use?
46. Do you have a content marketing strategy?
47. How do you collect and use customer feedback?
48. What is your customer retention rate?

## 6. Customer Data & Satisfaction
49. How many active customers do you currently have?
50. What is your net promoter score (NPS) or customer satisfaction score?
51. What are the most common customer complaints or feedback?
52. How do you collect customer feedback?
53. What is your customer churn rate?
54. How do you handle customer support?
55. What is your average customer support response time?
56. Do you have a customer loyalty or retention program?
57. What is the average order value from your customers?
58. What percentage of revenue comes from repeat customers?

## 7. Competition & Market Data
59. Who do you consider your main competitors?
60. How do you position yourself against competitors?
61. What market trends are affecting your business?
62. What market opportunities are you exploring?
63. How do you stay updated on industry news and changes?
64. What is your market share in your local area/industry?
65. Are you planning to expand to new markets?
66. What barriers to entry exist in your industry?

## 8. Growth & Scaling Plans
67. Are you currently looking to scale your business? (Y/N)
68. What are your plans for business expansion?
69. Do you need help with fundraising?
70. Are you considering partnerships or collaborations?
71. Do you plan to add new products or services?
72. Are you looking to hire more employees?
73. Are you exploring new geographic markets?
74. Do you have plans to implement automation?
75. Are you considering mergers or acquisitions?

## 9. Operations & Process Efficiency
76. What are the main operational challenges you face?
77. How do you currently manage your business processes?
78. Do you use any workflow management tools?
79. What processes are most time-consuming in your business?
80. How do you manage inventory (if applicable)?
81. What is your average order fulfillment time?
82. How do you handle quality control?
83. Do you have standard operating procedures documented?
84. Are there any processes you'd like to outsource?
85. How do you measure operational efficiency?

## 10. Human Resources & Culture
86. How do you currently handle employee recruitment?
87. What is your employee retention rate?
88. Do you have an employee satisfaction tracking system?
89. What is your company culture like?
90. How do you handle employee training and development?
91. Do you offer any employee benefits beyond basic requirements?
92. How do you handle conflict resolution in the workplace?
93. Do you have performance management systems in place?
94. How do you encourage innovation and creativity in your team?
95. What are your biggest HR challenges?

## 11. Legal & Compliance
96. What legal structure is your business? (LLC, Corp, etc.)
97. Do you have any patents or intellectual property?
98. What licenses or permits does your business require?
99. How do you handle data privacy and GDPR compliance?
100. Do you have any pending legal issues or concerns?
101. Do you have appropriate business insurance?
102. How do you handle contracts with customers/suppliers?
103. Are you compliant with industry-specific regulations?
104. Who is your legal counsel or advisor?

## 12. Future Technology Needs
105. What business problems would you solve with AI/automation?
106. Are you interested in implementing chatbots for customer service?
107. Do you need help with data analytics and insights?
108. Are you interested in predictive analytics for your business?
109. Would you benefit from automated reporting systems?
110. Are you looking to implement machine learning for any processes?
111. Do you need help with digital transformation?
112. Are you interested in using AI for customer insights?
113. What specific technology improvements would you prioritize?
114. Are you open to implementing new software solutions?
