## Template 1: Initial Business Contact Form

### Form Title: Business Information & Needs Assessment

### Description:
"We're excited to learn more about your business and how we can help you achieve your growth goals. This brief form will help us understand your needs and customize our approach to best serve you."

### Questions:

#### Section 1: Basic Business Information
1. **Business Name** (Short answer, required)
2. **Primary Contact Name** (Short answer, required)  
3. **Job Title** (Short answer, required)
4. **Business Email** (Short answer, required, validation: email)
5. **Business Phone** (Short answer, required)
6. **Industry** (Multiple choice, required)
   - Technology
   - Healthcare
   - Finance
   - Retail/E-commerce
   - Manufacturing
   - Professional Services
   - Education
   - Other (please specify)
7. **Business Location (City, State)** (Short answer, required)
8. **Years in Operation** (Multiple choice, required)
   - Less than 1 year
   - 1-3 years
   - 4-7 years
   - 8-15 years
   - Over 15 years
9. **Company Size** (Multiple choice, required)
   - Solo entrepreneur
   - 2-10 employees
   - 11-50 employees
   - 51-200 employees
   - 201-500 employees
   - Over 500 employees

#### Section 2: Business Goals & Challenges
10. **What are your primary business goals for the next 12 months?** (Paragraph, required)
11. **What is the biggest challenge your business is currently facing?** (Paragraph, required)  
12. **How did you hear about our consulting services?** (Multiple choice)
    - Referral
    - Search engine
    - Social media
    - Industry event
    - Other
13. **Best time to contact you** (Multiple choice)
    - Morning (8am-12pm)
    - Afternoon (12pm-5pm)
    - Evening (5pm-8pm)
    - No preference
14. **How soon would you like to start working with a consultant?** (Multiple choice)
    - Immediately
    - Within 1 month
    - Within 3 months
    - Sometime next quarter
    - Just researching options
