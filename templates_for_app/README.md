# Business Consulting Templates

This folder contains ready-made templates that you can import into the Business Consulting Toolkit app.

## Available Templates

1. **AI Readiness Assessment Template** - For evaluating a business's AI readiness and opportunities
2. **Operations Efficiency Report Template** - For analyzing operational efficiency and bottlenecks
3. **E-commerce Diagnostic Template** - For e-commerce businesses to evaluate performance
4. **SaaS Business Assessment Template** - For SaaS businesses to evaluate metrics and growth
5. **General Business Assessment Template** - For general business evaluation

## How to Import Templates into the App

1. Open the Business Consulting Toolkit app in your browser
2. Go to the Templates tab
3. Click the "Import Template" button
4. Select one of the JSON template files from this folder
5. The template will be loaded into your app and available for use

## Using the Templates

1. After importing a template, you can use it in the Reports section
2. Select a customer and the imported template
3. Fill in the placeholder values with actual business data
4. Generate the report

## Template Placeholders

Each template contains placeholders in the format {{placeholder_name}} which need to be filled with actual business data when generating reports.
