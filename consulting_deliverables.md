# AI Consulting Business Deliverables

- AI implementation opportunities assessment
- Current technology stack evaluation
- Priority recommendations (3-5 key actions)
- ROI projections for top recommendations
- 30-minute video call to discuss findings

**Timeline**: 1-3 days after data collection

**Template Structure**:
- Executive Summary
- Current State Assessment
- AI Opportunity Analysis
- Recommendations with ROI
- Implementation Roadmap
- Next Steps
